Tidal Client

Tidal Client is a launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition on Windows. You sign in with your own Microsoft account, it downloads the game from Mojang, and it starts Minecraft for you. You can also add Fabric mods, resource packs and shaders from Modrinth, or your own files.

We don't run any servers for the launcher. There are no Tidal accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your sign-in details stay on your PC.

How sign-in works

Tidal uses the Microsoft device code flow. The launcher shows a short code, you enter it on microsoft.com and approve the sign-in there. Tidal never sees your password. It asks for the XboxLive.signin and offline_access permissions, exchanges the result for an Xbox Live and then a Minecraft token, and checks that the account owns Minecraft: Java Edition. Tokens are stored on your PC in a file encrypted with the Windows key store.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: Tidal Client has no servers of its own, no Tidal accounts, no analytics and no ads. We don't collect your data. What the launcher needs is kept on your PC, and the only places it talks to are the services listed below.

What is stored on your PC

  • Your Minecraft account name and ID, and the sign-in tokens Microsoft and Mojang give the launcher.
  • Your settings: memory, Minecraft version, mod loader, text font and typing sound.
  • Game files, mods, packs, and any files you add in My uploads, with the name, picture and description you gave them.

Account details, tokens and settings are kept in one file encrypted with the Windows key store (the same protection Windows uses for its own saved credentials). Everything lives in %APPDATA%\tidal-client. We never see your Microsoft password, because you enter it on microsoft.com, not in Tidal.

Who the launcher connects to

The launcher makes requests to these services to do its job. Like any website or app, each of them can see your IP address, and each has its own privacy policy.

  • Microsoft and Xbox Live (login.microsoftonline.com, xboxlive.com): to sign you in. Your tokens are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services.
  • Mojang and Minecraft services (api.minecraftservices.com, api.mojang.com, sessionserver.mojang.com, textures.minecraft.net): to log in to Minecraft, check you own the game, look up skins, and change your skin if you ask.
  • Mojang download servers (piston-meta.mojang.com, launchermeta.mojang.com, resources.download.minecraft.net): to download the game, its libraries, assets and Java.
  • Fabric (meta.fabricmc.net, maven.fabricmc.net): to install the Fabric mod loader if you choose it.
  • Modrinth (api.modrinth.com, cdn.modrinth.com): to search for and download mods, packs and shaders when you use Browse.
  • GitHub (github.com): to check for and download Tidal updates.

When you start Minecraft, the launcher passes your session token to the game on your PC so it can join servers. This is how every Minecraft launcher works.

What we collect

Nothing. Tidal doesn't send us usage data, crash reports or logs, and we have no server that could receive them. This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages and doesn't use cookies or trackers. GitHub may keep its own server logs of visits.

Files you add

Files you add in My uploads are copied to a folder on your PC. They are scanned there and are never sent to us or to anyone else by Tidal.

Sharing and selling

We don't have your data, so we can't sell it or share it.

Deleting your data

  • In Tidal, go to Security and choose Wipe data to remove your accounts, tokens and settings.
  • To remove everything, including downloaded game files and uploads, uninstall Tidal and delete the %APPDATA%\tidal-client folder.
  • To cut off Tidal's access to your Microsoft account, remove it from the app permissions list at account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Children

Tidal Client isn't aimed at children under 13. Accounts for younger players are managed through Microsoft family settings, and we collect no personal information from anyone.
